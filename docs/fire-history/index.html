Data and method

BDIFF gives the commune where each fire started, not its exact position. Fire starts are spread evenly over the hexagons of that commune (1 hexagon = 1,500 ha). A fire's whole area is credited to the commune where it started, even when it spread into its neighbours: Landiras 2022 also burned Guillos, Hostens and nearby communes. Zone figures split each commune between zones by its share of hexagons.

Fire causes are recorded for less than half of the fires. The zones and the massif outline are the same as on the forest map.
