How this map is made

The massif is drawn from 277 real communes of Gironde, Landes and Lot-et-Garonne, about 1.33 million hectares. That matches the national forest inventory, which counts 987,950 ha of forest at 74.3% cover. I split it into nine zones following the old natural regions ("pays").

Totals for the whole massif follow published figures where they exist and are estimated between them. How each total splits between zones is my estimate, based on when each area was planted, where the 1940s fires and the storms hit hardest, and where farm clearings happened. Inside a zone, which hexagons are green is illustrative; the number of green hexagons is what the figures say. Fire hexagons sit around the real fire locations.
